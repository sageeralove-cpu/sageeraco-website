Hi, I'm Jackie — an ICU nurse building tools I wish I'd had.

Sage Era Co started with a simple question: why is it so hard to know what you're actually owed? Award pay is confusing. Rosters are irregular. And nurses are tired.

So I started building — at home, between day sleeps and night shifts, with ideas sometimes arriving at 3am when my brain wouldn't switch off after a shift. These tools are the result of those quiet, stubborn little hours.
